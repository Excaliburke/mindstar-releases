# Mindstar

Mindstar helps you understand your own medical record. Ask questions about your visits, medicines, tests and bills, and every answer shows the page of your record it came from.

**Private.** Your records, your questions and Mindstar's answers stay on your device.
**Not medical advice.** Mindstar reads your records back to you. For decisions about your care, talk to your care team.

## Download

Get the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/Excaliburke/mindstar-releases/releases/latest)**: open `Mindstar-<version>.dmg` and drag Mindstar to Applications.

- Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or newer.
- The first time it runs, Mindstar downloads its medical model: about 4.6 GB on 16 GB devices, 10.3 GB on 24-36 GB, and 18.7 GB on 48 GB and up. After that it works offline.
- Signed and notarized by Apple.

This is a **tester preview**. Please send feedback to the person who shared it with you.
